Settings guide · iOS 27

iPhone & iPad parental controls

Apple's parental controls are built into every iPhone and iPad, with nothing to download. They were redesigned for iOS 27. Here's what each setting does now and the order I'd set them up in.

FreeBuilt in. No app or subscription needed.
iOS 27New menus and names. Update every device first.
Parent-ledEverything is managed from your own iPhone.
OffMost controls do nothing until you set them up.

1Before you start

  1. Set up Family SharingGive your child their own child account, not your Apple Account.
  2. Update to iOS 27Update every device in the family group (iPadOS 27, macOS 27, watchOS 27 too).
  3. Add yourself to their contactsSo their requests for time, apps and websites reach you.
  4. Open the controlsOn your iPhone: Settings → Family → [child's name]
Older devices? A device that can't update to iOS 27 keeps the iOS 26 controls under Settings → Screen Time, with the older names (Downtime, App Limits, Communication Limits).

2Apps, purchases and websites

Ask to Buy

Apps & Websites → Restrictions → Ask to Buy → turn on Require Purchase Approval.

Your child sends you a request for every download and purchase. You approve or decline from your phone.

Ask to Browse

Apps & Websites → Restrictions → Filtering → Approved Websites Only.

Your child asks before visiting a new website in Safari and other supported browsers. On by default for under-13s.

Block an app

In Apps & Websites, pick an app and tap Block App. It isn't deleted, just greyed out on their Home Screen.

Age ratings

In Content & Privacy Restrictions, set the maximum age rating for apps, films, TV and music. Limit Adult Websites is on by default for under-18s.

3Screen time

Time Allowances

Replaces App Limits. Tap Time Allowances → Add to set daily limits for categories such as Games, Social Media and Entertainment, plus an overall daily budget.

Screen Time Schedule

Replaces Downtime. Choose which apps work at different times, with separate weekday and weekend routines. Ideal for bedtime and school hours.

Your child can ask for more time, and you can approve it from your phone. For teens, limits by category are usually fairer than locking the whole phone.

4Who can contact them

Allowed Contacts

Allowed Contacts → During Screen Time → Contacts Only, Contacts and Groups with at Least One Contact, or Everyone.

Turn on Manage Contacts to approve contacts yourself. Messages from anyone new stay hidden until you approve them. Leave Allow Contact Editing off if you want them to ask before adding people.

Communication Safety

Communication Safety → turn on.

On your child's device it blurs or warns about photos and videos showing nudity, gore or violence, including in Messages and FaceTime. Apple says the checking happens on the device itself.

These limits cover Apple's Phone, Messages and FaceTime only. WhatsApp, Snapchat, Instagram and other apps need their own privacy settings. On your child's iPhone, Settings → Apps → Messages → Screen Unknown Senders also keeps texts from unknown numbers out of the main list.

5Talk about it

Conversation starters

  • What apps do you think are fair to have time limits on?
  • If you need more time for homework, ask me and I'll sort it.
  • If someone you don't know messages you, show me. You won't be in trouble.

Signs to watch for

  • Turning the screen away or closing apps when you walk past
  • Being upset or withdrawn after using their phone
  • New contacts or "online friends" they won't talk about
  • Messages at unusual hours, or unexplained purchase requests
Set the controls up with your child, not behind their back, and explain why. Start tighter for younger children and loosen things as they show they're ready. The settings are a safety net, not a replacement for talking.

6Quick checklist

  • Family Sharing with a child account
  • Every device updated to iOS 27
  • Ask to Buy: Require Purchase Approval on
  • Ask to Browse or Limit Adult Websites set
  • Time Allowances and a Screen Time Schedule
  • Allowed Contacts and Manage Contacts set
  • Communication Safety on
  • Privacy settings checked in WhatsApp, Snapchat and others
