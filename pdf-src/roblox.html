Roblox: what parents need to know

Roblox is one of the most popular places for children to play online. It's mostly games made by other users, with chat, so strangers, scams and spending are the main risks. Here's what changed in 2026 and the settings I'd use.

1What children do on Roblox

Play

Millions of games made by other users, on phones, tablets, consoles and computers.

Create

Build their own games and dress up avatars.

Chat and spend

Chat with friends and buy avatar items, game passes and private servers with Robux.

2The age checks for chat

How it works

Since early January 2026, anyone who wants to use chat has to complete an age check first, usually a facial age estimate using the device camera. Over-13s can use ID instead. Roblox says the images are processed by a company called Persona and deleted straight afterwards.

Who can chat with whom

Players are put into age groups: under 9, 9 to 12, 13 to 15, 16 to 17, 18 to 20 and 21+. They can only chat with similar ages. A 12-year-old can chat with players up to 15, and nobody 16 or over can chat with them in games. Chat is off by default for under-9s unless a parent agrees.

A welcome change, but not perfect. Age estimates can be wrong or fooled, and over-13s can still chat freely with "Trusted Connections". Keep the conversation going.

3The risks

Contact with strangers

Predators have used Roblox to befriend children and then move the chat to other apps. Roblox faces many lawsuits over this.

Scams

Fake "free Robux" offers, trading tricks and phishing links are common. Children can lose items or access to their account.

Spending

Robux costs real money and it's easy to spend a lot without realising. Illegal Roblox gambling sites have also let children bet Robux with no age checks.

Unsuitable games

Games are made by users, and some are violent, scary or sexual despite the rules, including games designed for online dating.

4What I'd set up

  1. Link your account to theirsCreate your own Roblox account and link it to your child's through Parental Controls. You can then manage screen time, friends, spending and settings from your own phone.
  2. Check their age and content levelMake sure their date of birth is correct and set the maximum content maturity for their age.
  3. Check chatReview who can chat with and message them in Parental Controls.
  4. Limit spendingSet a monthly spending limit, or zero. Remove saved cards from the device's app store account too.
Show them how to block and report, and make sure they know they won't be in trouble for telling you something.

5Talk about it

Conversation starters

  • What's the best game you've played on Roblox this week?
  • Has anyone ever offered you free Robux?
  • What would you do if someone asked you to chat somewhere else?

Signs something's wrong

  • Talking to an "online friend" in private, or being asked to move to another app
  • Wanting lots of Robux or real money, or mysterious new items
  • Getting very upset when asked to stop playing
  • Being secretive about who they play with
  • Being bullied, or bullying others, in games
