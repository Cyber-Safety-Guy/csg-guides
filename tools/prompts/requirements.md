CSG uses none. Ever. Replace every em dash with a comma, full stop, colon, or restructured sentence. This is a hard rule.</p><p><strong>Title case in headings</strong></p><p>AI defaults to capitalising every main word in headings. CSG uses sentence case. "Why parents need to know about TikTok" not "Why Parents Need To Know About TikTok."</p><p><strong>Overuse of bold</strong></p><p>AI bolds keywords in a mechanical "key takeaways" style. Bold is used sparingly in CSG content — only when a word or phrase truly carries the weight of the sentence.</p><p><strong>Rule of three stacking</strong></p><p>AI loves "adjective, adjective, and adjective" or "phrase, phrase, and phrase" to appear comprehensive. Use this pattern only when the three things are genuinely distinct and all worth saying.</p><p><strong>Participial openers</strong></p><p>Never open a sentence with a present participle unless it's natural: "Building on this...", "Recognising that...", "Drawing from...". These read as AI warm-up phrases.</p><p><strong>Puffery and false significance</strong></p><p>Do not add statements about how arbitrary aspects of the topic represent or contribute to a broader movement.
